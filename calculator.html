Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

How does an Age Calculator work?

It compares your date of birth with a reference date and counts the completed years, then the completed months, then the remaining days, using the real calendar.

How can I calculate my exact age?

Enter your date of birth, keep today's date as the reference date, and press Calculate Age. You will see your age in years, months and days.

What is the difference between years, months, and days?

Years are the full birthdays you have completed. Months are the full months since your last birthday. Days are what remains after that.

Does this calculator support leap years?

Yes. Leap years are handled correctly, including February 29 birthdays. You can choose February 28 or March 1 as the birthday in non-leap years.

Can I calculate my age on a past date?

Yes. Choose any past date in the "Calculate Age As Of" field, as long as it is not earlier than your birth date.

Can I calculate my future age?

Yes. Select a future date to see how old you will be on that day.

Is my personal information stored?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser. Your date of birth is never sent to a server or saved.

How accurate is this Age Calculator?

It uses date-only calendar calculations with real month lengths and leap years, so it avoids timezone and rounding errors.
